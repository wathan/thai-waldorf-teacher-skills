## Which language

Read the class profile's language of instruction and language of student materials (with no profile: Thai for both, stated as an assumption).

| Profile setting | Student-facing supports |
|---|---|
| Student materials in Thai | Thai, in scientific register (ภาษาวิชาการ). For students learning in Thai as an additional language, add a picture or English gloss beside each key Thai word and frames at the most supported level. |
| Student materials in English | English academic register. For students learning in English as an additional language, add the Thai term (or the home language, as the teacher can provide) beside each key word. |
| Bilingual | Both, side by side, Thai official terms first. |

Thai scientific terms follow the official wording: take indicator verbs from the Thai science glossary read in Step 0 (บรรยาย describe, อธิบาย explain with reasons and evidence, อภิปราย discuss) and use the indicator's own terms. Never name a student's home language unless the profile or teacher does.

## Language demands

For each task that carries the learning intention (usually the practice and the written or spoken explanation), note briefly:

- **Vocabulary:** the words the task assumes (see tiers below).
- **Grammar:** cause and effect (เพราะ…จึง…, ทำให้…; because…, so…), comparison (มากกว่า…, ในขณะที่…; whereas…), hedging (อาจ…, ข้อมูลชี้ว่า…; suggests that…), passive and nominalised forms in English (the movement of air; evaporation).
- **Discourse:** the structure the task needs: claim, evidence and reasoning; sequence of a process; compare and contrast.
- **Genre and register:** explanation, report or argument; impersonal academic register in the main lesson book versus everyday talk in pair work. Students who speak a language fluently in conversation may still lack its academic register.

Then name the two or three highest-impact supports and where in the session they go (before the task, during, at the close).

## Vocabulary tiers

| Tier | What it is | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1: everyday | common words; for additional-language learners, also idioms, words with several meanings and culturally specific terms | gloss or picture only where needed |
| Tier 2: academic, cross-subject | e.g. วิเคราะห์, เปรียบเทียบ, หลักฐาน, ปัจจัย, ความสัมพันธ์, ผลกระทบ; analyse, evidence, factor, affect, pattern | the priority for explicit teaching: a student-friendly meaning, the word in this task's context, revisit across the week |
| Tier 3: technical | the science terms of the unit, e.g. ประชากร, ระบบนิเวศ, ผู้ผลิต, อัตราการเกิดปฏิกิริยา, การชนกันของอนุภาค; latitude, convection, Hadley cell | taught through the experience and concept formation, written with a picture or diagram on a word wall or in the main lesson book |

Choose five to eight priority words per task, mostly Tier 2 and the Tier 3 words the learning intention depends on. In a main lesson block, technical terms arrive with concept formation after the experience, not as a word list before the class has met the phenomenon.

## Sentence frames

Frames encode the thinking of the task, not just its grammar. Give them at up to three levels so students can choose:

| Level | Thai example (explaining a cause) | English example |
|---|---|---|
| Most supported | ข้อมูลแสดงว่า ______ ลดลง เพราะ ______ | The data show that ______ decreased because ______. |
| Supported | เมื่อ ______ เปลี่ยนไป ทำให้ ______ ดังนั้น ______ | When ______ changed, ______, so ______. |
| Light | Use: เพราะ, ทำให้, ดังนั้น, ในขณะที่, หลักฐานที่สนับสนุนคือ | Use: because, as a result, whereas, this suggests, the evidence for this is |

Frames by thinking move:

- **Observing and describing (บรรยาย):** ฉันสังเกตเห็นว่า … / I noticed that …
- **Explaining with evidence (อธิบาย):** … เกิดขึ้นเพราะ … หลักฐานคือ … / … happens because … The evidence is …
- **Comparing:** … แตกต่างจาก … ตรงที่ … / … differs from … in that …
- **Predicting:** ถ้า … เพิ่มขึ้น ฉันคาดว่า … เพราะ … / If … increased, I predict … because …
- **Taking a position in concept formation:** ฉันคิดว่า … เพราะ … / I think … because …
- **Building on or questioning another's idea:** ฉันเห็นด้วย/ไม่เห็นด้วยกับ … เพราะ … / I agree/disagree with … because …
- **Concluding a fair test:** เมื่อ … สูงขึ้น … เร็วขึ้น เพราะ … / As … rose, … became faster because …

Write frames for this task's content (the actual variables and phenomenon), and give speaking frames for pair talk as well as writing frames for the main lesson book.

## Using frames without lowering the thinking

- A frame leaves the reasoning to the student: never fill in the cause, the evidence or the conclusion.
- Do not turn frames into a gap-fill worksheet; offer them on a card or the board, used by choice.
- Talk before writing: rehearse the explanation aloud with a partner, then write.
- Fade over the main lesson block: fewer and lighter frames as the weeks go on; say in the plan when they fade.
- Scaffolds change the route, not the destination: no simpler question, fewer steps of reasoning, multiple-choice replacement of an explanation, or a different goal for students learning in an additional language.
