## Shape

Walk the session's own parts in teaching order, each part's name and time as a heading, every finding under the part it belongs to. Leave out a part with nothing to prepare (e.g. Maths/Literacy marked `—`). Keep to at most 500 words.

```markdown
# Preparation: Week 1, day 3 (Wed), ecology

From the day plan for week 1, day 3. 100 minutes.

**Rehearse first:** the opening Recall question, and lighting the burner under the pond-water flask.

## Rhythmic Time (10 min)
- Verse on the board before students arrive.

## Recall and concept formation (45 min): yesterday's pond-water samples
Working toward (not told): living things in a pond depend on each other and on the water's conditions.

| Students may say | Move | Say |
|---|---|---|
| "The fish died because the water was dirty." | Press for evidence | "What did you see in the samples that fits 'dirty'? What else could it be?" |
| … | … | … |

- Quieter channel: everyone sketches their position before pairs.
- Watch the clock: start the reflection by minute 50.

## Practice (25 min)
- Stuck: "Pick one creature from your drawing. What does it need?"
- Done early: "What would change for it in the hot season?"

## New experience (20 min): warming pond water, left un-analysed
- Likely: "Why are bubbles coming out?" Say: "Write that question down; we start there tomorrow."

## Equipment and setup
- [ ] Flask, tripod, gauze, burner, pond water collected this morning, …
- [ ] Dry run of the heating this afternoon.

## Risk notes
- Burner and hot glass: teacher only; heat-proof mat; students one metre back; let glass cool before moving.
- …
- If no bubbles show: …
```

Headings for a 5E session use its phases. A note "from your description" says so under the title.

## Timings

- Take each part's time from the plan; give clock times only if the plan has them.
- If the parts do not add up to the session length, show both and flag it in the reply.
- Add one "watch the clock" line where a part usually overruns (Recall and concept formation most of all), naming what to shorten so today's new experience still gets its full time.

## Equipment and setup

- Everything in the plan's Preparation cells, as a tick list, grouped by part when it helps.
- What Recall needs from yesterday: photos of the board or of main lesson book drawings, the previous day's demonstration set up again for reference, printed data.
- Setup that takes time or must happen earlier (water to heat, ice to freeze, a dry run, room layout, sight lines for a demonstration, copies to print), with when to do it.
- Name only what the plan implies; mark anything you add ("added: towel").

## Risk notes

For every demonstration, experiment, fieldwork or use of heat, flame, glass, pressure, electricity, chemicals, sharp tools, food or plants (allergies), or outdoor heat and sun:

- **Hazard → control**, one line each: e.g. "Mains-powered lamp over a water tank: cable routed away from the water; plug in only with dry hands; spills wiped at once."
- **Who handles what:** teacher demonstration or student hands-on, and the distance students keep.
- **If it fails:** a fallback that keeps the experience un-analysed (repeat once, a photo or short video, or carry the experience to tomorrow), never an explanation instead.
- **Rehearse:** a dry run the day before for anything that has not been done with this equipment.
- Follow the school's own safety rules; for chemicals, check the label or safety data sheet. Do not invent regulations or quote limits you cannot source.

No risk notes are needed for a session with no practical work; say "No practical work today."
