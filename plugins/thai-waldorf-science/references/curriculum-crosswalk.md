## 1. Grade bands

Thai ม. grades serve as the bands; each is paired with its Waldorf grade (assumed mapping in the macro curriculum) and that grade mode's own words.

| Thai grade | Waldorf grade mode | Question | Capacity | Motif (localise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ม.3 | G9 *What?* | What is here? What happened? What is actually going on? | Observation. Exact, theory-free description of phenomena as given, *before* explanation. Trust in one's own perception is *cultivated*, not assumed. | Unresolved opposition, e.g. heat/cold, expansion/contraction, revolution/old order, colliding plates. | ม.4 | G10 *How?* | How does this relate to that? How do opposing things interact, and what do they produce together? | Comparison. Weigh contrasting phenomena for their relative value, origin and significance. **Holistic thinking** emerges as grasping how opposites belong to one dynamic system. **Aesthetics** (balance, proportion, fit) is both felt and a cognitive tool. | Polarity and intensification, e.g. acid/base, centripetal/centrifugal, high/low pressure. | ม.5 | G11 *Why?* | Why are things this way? At its deepest reach this means destiny, meaning and responsibility, where it shades into “Who?” (§4). | Analysis. Independent, abstract theorising: resolving a whole into its constituents. **Imagination** becomes theoretical: coherent mental models of the unperceivable (atom, field, cell). This is disciplined construction, possible *only* because of the observational (g9) and relational (g10) groundwork. | The invisible and the individual element, e.g. atomic structure, electricity, cell theory, the periodic table, the solitary quester (Grail, Renaissance), the point at infinity in projective geometry. |
| ม.6 | G12 *Who?* | Who is this being called human? Who or what stands behind the whole? Pursued far enough it becomes “Why?” (§4). | Synthesis. Draw previously analysed, separate elements and disciplines into a coherent whole. **Holistic thinking** becomes explicit and conscious. **Imagination** is at its widest: the planet, the species, the epoch or one's whole biography as a single image. | Comprehensive synthesis, e.g. world history, a survey of architecture, the animal kingdom culminating in the human being, biochemistry reuniting grade 11's elements. |
