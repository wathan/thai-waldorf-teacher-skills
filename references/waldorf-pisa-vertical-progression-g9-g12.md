## Vertical notes (G12 frozen — candidates to raise, not changes)

1. **G12 cites the G11 Judgement row as its own.** The 06-17 draft cites
   "Judgement, grade 12 — 'limits of this model'" for S1.2 / S1.3, but that
   phrase is the macro **Grade 11** Judgement row ("Which model best explains
   the evidence — and what are the limits of this model?"). The G12 Judgement
   row is synthetic/ethical. Effect: G11 S1.4 / S2.4 now own "limits"
   natively, and G12 S1.3 is distinguished from G11 mainly by *regime choice
   across an integrated grasp of models*. Candidate: re-cite as "G11
   carried forward", as the draft already does for S1.2 in part.
2. **"No reaching up" is stricter than the macro.** The macro's cross-cutting
   note treats the G11/G12 Why/Who boundary as soft ("legitimately placeable
   in either year"). The personal frameworks resolve it by allowing
   Who-flavoured material only as an optional stretch in G11 evidence-shapes,
   never in statements.
3. **G12 Adds have no precursor sub-ability.** S1.7 (own understanding's
   trajectory) has only a soft G11 precursor (S1.4's self-as-constructor
   clause). S2.5 (single-study limits; integrating across studies) has none;
   planners wanting a ramp can seed it through G11 S2.4 evidence-shapes.
4. **G12 S1.4 now includes construction.** After the working group's
   suggestion ("Construct and hold …"), G12 S1.4 builds models *and* holds
   several in relation. The step from G11 S1.4 (construct *one* model of
   the unseen, evaluate it against a rival, see it as a construction) is
   from single-model construction to multi-model holding.
5. **G12 S2.2 is Keep, G10 and G11 Deepen it.** A design-proposal ramp exists
   below G12 (fair comparison → testing the unseen) but not at G12 itself.
   This is consistent (G12's S2 effort goes to S2.3 / S2.5), but a planner
   should not read G12 S2.2 as lighter than G11 S2.2.
6. **Ethical ladder across grades** (S3.5, E2.3, E3.1, E3.2, E3.4): empathy →
   critical → owned. G10 E3.1 / E3.2 stay Keep: PISA wording already fits the
   rung, so the ladder enters E3 only at E3.4 in G10.
   **No false balance holds on every rung:** G10 empathy is for the people
   and concerns behind a position, not parity for its factual claims; at G11
   a claim left out *because it failed evidential scrutiny* is a legitimate
   exclusion, not a silenced voice — the critical question is whether
   exclusions were for evidential reasons or for power.
7. **S2.4 is one representation thread; the object shifts.** A model is a
   representation — of the system or mechanism behind the data, where a
   graph or table represents the data themselves. G10 weighs *data*
   representations: which best reveals the relationship, which obscures it.
   G11 weighs *explanatory* representations (models): which best explains
   the data, and where it stops holding — its limits are what it obscures.
   G12 integrates both kinds into one coherent picture, knowing what each
   reveals and obscures (shared terms with G12 S1.2). Difference to keep in
   view: data representations *display*; models *explain and predict*, so
   G11's judgement is "best explains", not "best reveals".
8. **G9 sits below PISA's calibration; its Shifts are precursors.** G9 keeps
   only bullets already within descriptive reach (S1.1, S2.1, S3.2, S3.3,
   E1.2, E1.3) and shifts the rest *down*. A G10 Keep is therefore a real step
   up from its G9 precursor (e.g. S2.2: plan a full observation → set up a
   fair comparison), not a repeat.
9. **Ethical ladder now has four rungs:** witness → empathy → critical →
   owned. G9 faithful witness (S3.5, E2.3, E3.2, E3.4) describes each party
   accurately with no comparison; G10 empathy enters each perspective and
   then compares. No false balance holds at G9 as reporting a factual claim
   *as a claim*, kept apart from what is observed or established.
10. **S2.6 is a G9-only Add with no named successor.** Observed vs inferred
    is presupposed by G10 S2.4 (reading relationships, not reading them in),
    G11 S1.2 / S1.4 (a representation or model as a construction rather than
    a transcription), and G12 S2.5 (what a single study can establish), but
    no later grade restates it. Its number is the next free one across all
    grades because G12 holds S2.5.
11. **Unresolved → resolved opposition.** The G9 motif (opposition
    experienced and described, not yet resolved) feeds G10 polarity: G9 E1.1
    describes opposing processes side by side; G10 E1.1 grasps them as one
    dynamic system. Similarly G9 S1.5 only *recognises* hypotheses; G10
    first *develops* them.
12. **G9 E2 and E3 are near-term and local.** Future thinking G9
    ("What is happening to me, right now?") limits E2.2, E2.4 and E3.3 to
    concrete, near-term action. Future generations and the range of scales
    from local to global enter at G10; long-range orientation at G12.
