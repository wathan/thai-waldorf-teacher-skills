# Day plan: week 1, day 2 (Tue)

Unit 1: Air on the move. Session: 100 minutes.

## Objective

Work toward explaining what changed in the air before the storm

## Sequence

1. Rhythmic time (10 min)
2. Recall + concept formation: sketch an initial model of the storm, compare models (35 min)
3. Students write questions; driving question board (20 min)
4. Practice: label the sketch models (15 min)
5. New experience: convection tank (heated water with dye) (15 min)
6. Close (5 min)

## Differentiation

Sketch or write the recall; key words in English and Thai.
