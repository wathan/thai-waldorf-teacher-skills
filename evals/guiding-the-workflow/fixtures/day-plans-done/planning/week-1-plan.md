# Week 1 plan: Air on the move

| Day | Objectives | Main Lesson | Differentiation | Preparation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon (day 1) | Connect to the G11 atmosphere block; meet the anchoring phenomenon | Link to previous block: what the class saw of clouds and sky colour in G11 · Preview of the journey · Practice: set up main lesson books · New experience: time-lapse video and rain-gauge data of the first monsoon storm over Chiang Mai, after a week of haze photos · Close: tomorrow starts from what you saw | Video plus printed photos; key words in English and Thai | Video, rain-gauge printout, haze photos |
| Tue (day 2) | Work toward explaining what changed in the air before the storm | Recall + concept formation (yesterday: the monsoon storm): each student sketches an initial model of what the air did, the class compares models · Students write their own questions, then the class clusters them into the driving question board and rewords the driving question if it wants to · Reflection · Practice: label the storm sketch models · New experience: convection tank (heated water with dye) · Close | Sketch or write recall; sentence starters | Glass tank, heater, food dye |
| Wed (day 3) | Students learn the definition of convection | Recall (yesterday: convection tank) · Concept formation: the teacher explains convection and students copy the definition "Convection is the transfer of heat by the movement of a fluid" into their main lesson books · Reflection · Practice: revise the storm sketch models using the definition · New experience: sea-breeze box with a candle and incense smoke · Close | Definition given in English and Thai | Sea-breeze box, sand, ice, incense |
| Thu (day 4) | Work toward explaining why the coast has a daily breeze | Recall + concept formation (yesterday: sea-breeze box): individual explanations first, then the class builds a shared explanation · The class agrees its own criteria for "a good model of the storm" and records them in the main lesson books · Practice: groups decide which change to test in the sea-breeze box (ice, lamp, sand or water) and plan their own trial for Friday · New experience: turntable demo of a rolled ball tracing a curved path · Close | Data as table and graph | Turntable, chalk, ball |
| Fri (day 5) | Work toward explaining why large winds curve | Recall + concept formation (yesterday: turntable) · Reflection · Practice: groups run the sea-breeze trials they planned; then copy the global wind belts from the textbook diagram · New experience: Chiang Mai hourly wind direction over one day in April and one in July · Close: exit slip "How well can I now explain the storm? What is still unclear?" (assessment as learning, not scored) | Wind roses printed large; extension prompts | Wind data printouts, sea-breeze boxes |
