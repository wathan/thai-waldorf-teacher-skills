# Meteorology block plan

## Storyline design

- **Anchoring phenomenon** (chosen by the teacher): the arrival of the southwest monsoon over Chiang Mai: the first heavy afternoon storm after weeks of haze and heat.
- **Driving question** (teacher-drafted, provisional until the class revisits it at the driving question board): Why does the rain come back to Chiang Mai so suddenly every May?
- **Target ideas:** air moves from high to low pressure, driven by uneven heating; Earth's rotation turns large-scale winds into latitude belts; land and sea heat differently, and the seasonal shift of that contrast drives the monsoon.
- **Prerequisite ideas:** warm air is less dense than cool air; water vapour condenses when air cools (from the G11 block).
- **Units (fixed sequence):** 1 Air on the move (fixed) · 2 Ocean currents (fixed) · 3 Putting it together: the monsoon (fixed). The driving question board frames how each unit is introduced; it does not change the order.
- **Grade mode:** G12 Who?
- **Thai indicators:** ว 3.2 ม.6/10 อธิบายการหมุนเวียนของอากาศตามเขตละติจูด และผลที่มีต่อภูมิอากาศ · ว 3.2 ม.6/11 · ว 3.2 ม.6/12

| Week | Unit | Focus |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | Ocean currents | Wind-driven currents, heat carried by the sea |
