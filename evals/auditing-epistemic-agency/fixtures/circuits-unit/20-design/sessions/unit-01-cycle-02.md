# Unit 1, cycle 2: Ohm's law

Sessions 4–6 · 200 minutes · indicators `ว 2.3 ม.3/1`, `ว 2.3 ม.3/2`

## Thai core plan stages

- **ขั้นนำ:** retrieval practice on cycle 1 and Engage (session 4).
- **ขั้นสอน:** Explore (session 4), Explain and Elaborate (session 5).
- **ขั้นสรุป:** Evaluate, then the exit slip (session 6).

## Session 4 · Friday week 2 · 50 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retrieval practice | 10 | From memory: draw how a voltmeter and an ammeter are connected; then check with a partner (not scored). | Draw or write |
| Engage | 10 | Challenge: predict the current through a new resistor before switching on. Students write their prediction and reason. | Prediction frame |
| Explore | 30 | Groups test their predictions and record V and I for two resistors. | Table frame |

## Session 5 · Tuesday week 3 · 100 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explore | 20 | Groups plot both resistors on one graph and compare the slopes. | Graph frame |
| Explain | 30 | Groups explain the pattern in their own words; the class agrees on a statement; the teacher introduces V = IR from it. | Word wall |
| Explain | 20 | Explicit instruction: worked example of a V = IR calculation, then a faded example the class completes together. | Worked example card |
| Elaborate | 40 | Graded practice: calculations from one-step to two-step problems, then a household example. | Calculator; start at any step |

## Session 6 · Friday week 3 · 50 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluate | 35 | Practical task: predict, measure and calculate for an unknown resistor; written up in the notebook (scored with the rubric). | Choice of table or written report |
| Close | 15 | Exit slip: "How sure am I of V = IR, and why?" (assessment as learning, not scored). | Sentence starters |
