# Assessment plan: week 1 (Air on the move)

For the week 1 plan of the meteorology block. Learning intention: students build and revise their own model of why the first monsoon storm arrives so suddenly.

## Flags

None.

## Thu (day 4)

- **Assessment as learning: the class sets its own criteria.** After concept formation on the sea-breeze box, the class agrees its own criteria for "a good model of the storm" (what a good model must show and what counts as good evidence for it). Students copy the agreed criteria into their main lesson books. Channel: pairs first, then the whole class. Teacher next: keeps the class's wording as it stands; the criteria are used for every storm model for the rest of the block.

## Fri (day 5)

- **Assessment as learning: self-assessment against the class's criteria.** Students check their revised storm model against the criteria the class set on Thursday, in writing, privately. Not scored.
- **Exit slip:** "How well can I now explain the storm? What is still unclear?" Not scored.
