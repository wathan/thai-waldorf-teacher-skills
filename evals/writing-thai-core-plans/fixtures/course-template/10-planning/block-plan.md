# Weather block plan

## Storyline design

- **Anchoring phenomenon** (chosen by the teacher): the afternoon thunderstorm that builds over Doi Suthep on a hot May day while the city below stays dry.
- **Driving question** (teacher-drafted, provisional until the class revisits it at the driving question board): Why does the storm build over the mountain and not over the city?
- **Target ideas:** air moves from high to low pressure, driven by uneven heating; rising air cools and its water vapour condenses into cloud; slopes heated by the sun push air upward.
- **Prerequisite ideas:** land heats faster than water; warm air is less dense than cool air (from the G10 block).
- **Units (fixed sequence):** 1 Moving air (fixed) · 2 Rising air and clouds (fixed) · 3 Putting it together: the mountain storm (fixed).
- **Grade mode:** G11 Why?
- **Thai indicators:**
  - `ว 3.2 ม.6/8` อธิบายการหมุนเวียนของอากาศ ที่เป็นผลมาจากความแตกต่างของความกดอากาศ — sequencing difference: a ม.6 indicator taught in ม.5, because the G11 Why? mode asks for causes, and pressure differences are the cause the class is after.

| Week | Unit | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moving air | Uneven heating, pressure, local winds |
| 2 | Rising air and clouds | Cooling by rising, condensation, cloud types |
| 3 | Putting it together: the mountain storm | Slope heating and the afternoon storm; back to the driving question |
