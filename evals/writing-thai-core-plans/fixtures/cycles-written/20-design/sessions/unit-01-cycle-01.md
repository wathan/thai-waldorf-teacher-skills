# Unit 1, cycle 1: Measuring a circuit

Sessions 1–3 · 250 minutes · indicators `ว 2.3 ม.3/3`, `ว 2.3 ม.3/2`

## Thai core plan stages

- **ขั้นนำ:** Engage (session 1).
- **ขั้นสอน:** Explore (sessions 1–2), Explain (session 3), Elaborate with the hinge question from Evaluate steering it (session 3).
- **ขั้นสรุป:** students' own summary in their notebooks and the exit slip (session 3).

## Session 1 · Tuesday week 1 · 100 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engage | 15 | Two bulbs on the same battery glow differently. Students write what they notice and wonder, then share in pairs. | Picture prompt cards; say or write |
| Explore | 70 | Groups build simple circuits with different bulbs and resistors and record how brightly each glows; first attempt at measuring with the meters, guided by a picture card. | Picture card of meter connections; roles in the group |
| Explore | 15 | Groups record their observations in a shared table. | Table frame |

## Session 2 · Friday week 1 · 50 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explore | 10 | Retrieval practice: from memory, students draw last Tuesday's circuit and label where each meter went (not scored). | Draw or write |
| Explore | 40 | Groups measure current and voltage for one resistor at three battery settings and plot I against V. | Graph frame with axes drawn |

## Session 3 · Tuesday week 2 · 100 minutes

| Phase | Minutes | What happens | UDL options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explain | 30 | Groups present their graphs and their own explanations; the class compares them. The teacher then introduces the terms voltage, current and resistance with the class's graphs. | Word wall in Thai and English |
| Explain | 20 | Explicit instruction: the teacher models reading a voltmeter and an ammeter step by step (I do, we do, you do). | Step card |
| Evaluate | 10 | Hinge question: which meter is connected wrongly in these three diagrams? Answers on mini-whiteboards steer the next part (not scored). | Diagrams enlarged |
| Elaborate | 30 | Graded practice: measure an unknown component, from a fully guided sheet to an open task. | Start at any level of the sheet |
| Close | 10 | Students write their own summary; exit slip: "What I can now measure, and what is still unclear" (assessment as learning, not scored). | Sentence starters |
