# Meteorology block plan (draft)

Anchoring phenomenon (my choice): the arrival of the southwest monsoon over Chiang Mai: the first heavy afternoon storm after weeks of haze and heat.

Units I have in mind. I want the class's questions on the board to decide the order of the middle ones:

- The first storm (week 1, starts from the anchor)
- Air on the move (ว 3.2 ม.6/10)
- Ocean currents (ว 3.2 ม.6/11)
- Reading the sky and the weather map (ว 3.2 ม.6/14)
- Visit to the Northern Meteorological Center in Chiang Mai: booked for Wednesday of week 3, the only day they can host us
- Putting it together: the monsoon (end of week 4, ว 3.2 ม.6/12)
