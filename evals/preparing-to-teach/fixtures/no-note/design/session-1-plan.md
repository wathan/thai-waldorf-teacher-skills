# Session 1 (Thu 29 Oct): which cup keeps ice longest?

160 minutes, 13:00–15:40. 5E cycle. Cycle question: ถ้วยแบบไหนเก็บน้ำแข็งได้นานที่สุด แล้วเราจะรู้แน่ได้อย่างไร

Skills taught explicitly: ทักษะการกำหนดนิยามเชิงปฏิบัติการ, ทักษะการกำหนดและควบคุมตัวแปร

| Time | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 13:00–13:15 | Engage | Ice cube in the hand vs on the table; students say which melts first and why. |
| 13:15–13:40 | Explore: defining | Each student writes what "เก็บน้ำแข็งได้นานกว่า" means as something to measure, and lists what must stay the same, before the terms are taught. |
| 13:40–14:30 | Explore: measuring | Groups of three, fixed roles: foam, paper and steel cups, one ice cube each, mass of meltwater every 10 minutes. |
| 14:30–14:55 | Explore: graph and claim | Each student draws three lines on one graph and writes a claim with evidence. |
| 14:55–15:00 | Changeover | Pack away, face the board. |
| 15:00–15:25 | Explain | Class table of results; fast whole-class comparison; teacher introduces ตัวแปรต้น, ตัวแปรตาม, ตัวแปรที่ต้องควบคุมให้คงที่ with a worked example; hinge question. |
| 15:25–15:35 | Elaborate | New case: a cool box for a picnic; students name the three kinds of variable. |
| 15:35–15:40 | Evaluate | Exit slip against the success criteria. |
