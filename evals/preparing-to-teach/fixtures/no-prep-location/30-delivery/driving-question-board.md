# Driving question board

Driving question (teacher-drafted, provisional): Why does the rain come back to Chiang Mai so suddenly every May?

## Clusters after week 1

- **Where does the wind come from?** Why does the wind change direction between April and July? Why is the breeze at the beach cooler in the afternoon?
- **Where does the water come from?** Where does all the rain come from if it has not rained for months? Why are the storm clouds so tall?
- **Why so sudden?** Why does it change in just a few days?
