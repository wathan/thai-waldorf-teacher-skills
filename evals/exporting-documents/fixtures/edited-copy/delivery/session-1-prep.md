# Preparation: session 1 (Thu 29 Oct), which cup keeps ice longest

160 minutes.

**Rehearse:** weighing meltwater on the balance at 13:50, and the 15:00 class table.

## Engage (13:00–13:15)
Working toward (not told): what you measure decides what "longer" means.

| Students may say | Move | Say |
|---|---|---|
| "น้ำแข็งบนโต๊ะละลายช้ากว่า เพราะโต๊ะเย็น" | Press for evidence | "โต๊ะเย็นกว่ามือไหม เราจะรู้ได้อย่างไร" |

## Explore: defining (13:15–13:40)
- Write alone first, talk second.

## Explore: measuring (13:40–14:30)
- Balance zeroed with the empty beaker before every reading.

## Explore: graph and claim (14:30–14:55)
- Hold, don't correct: "ถ้วยโฟมทำให้น้ำแข็งเย็นขึ้น" (cold as a substance).

## Explain (15:00–15:25)
- **Watch the clock:** groups compare the class table first; shorten the worked example, not the comparison.

## Equipment and setup
- [ ] Per group ×5: foam, paper and steel cups, 3 ice cubes, balance, beaker, stopwatch
- [ ] 12:30: ice out of the freezer into the cool box

## Risk notes
- Wet floor from meltwater → trays under every cup; wipe at once.
- Supporting teacher → no equipment handling.
