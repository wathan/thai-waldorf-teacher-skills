# Block plan: Mechanics (G10, 3 weeks)

- **Driving question:** Why do things fall, swing and collide the way they do? (built from the class's questions on day 2)
- **Learning intention:** Students explain the motion of falling, rolling and colliding bodies using forces and momentum, supporting their explanations with their own measurements.
- **Anchoring phenomenon:** a long pendulum and a ball dropped beside it from the same height.

| Week | Unit | Target ideas |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Swinging | A pendulum's period depends on its length, not its mass |
| 2 | Falling and rolling | Without air resistance, all bodies fall with the same acceleration |
| 3 | Pushing and colliding | A net force changes motion; momentum is shared in a collision |
