# Mechanics block sheet (given to students on day 1)

## What we will work on

How things fall, swing and collide, and how forces and momentum explain them.

## Criteria for the main lesson book

1. Each experience recorded clearly in drawings and words.
2. Explanations use evidence from our own measurements.
3. Graphs and tables are labelled, with units.
4. The forces on a body are shown with arrows and named.
